### 2. Daytona — Vegas's Cousin Nobody's Covering

Daytona is a 2v2 format where each team pairs their two scores into a two-digit number (low score goes first). If your team makes a birdie and theirs doesn't, you get to "flip the bird" — reversing their score so the higher digit goes first. It's mechanically identical to Vegas but adds the birdie-flip rule.

We have a Vegas guide. Daytona is a named variant that generates its own searches (GolfCompendium has a dedicated page). This is either a Vegas guide expansion (add a "Daytona Variation" section) or a short standalone bridge piece.

**Why it matters:** The Vegas guide already notes pairing mechanics — Daytona is one FAQ away from capturing this search cluster. Very low effort.

**Suggested action:** Add "What is Daytona golf?" as an FAQ to the Vegas guide with a brief explanation and note that Stick's Vegas game tracks the same pairing logic. The Vegas guide already added a `daytona` keyword on May 4 — the FAQ is the missing piece.
